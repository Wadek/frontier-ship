# 400m campaign

- Muumi Mile through 31 Oct 2026 (race 18 Oct). Then 400m from 1 Nov.
- Default 10 weeks to Sat 10 Jan 2027. Advanced/expert may pick 8 weeks (27 Dec).
- `apply400mPlan` in `frontend/src/lib/muumi-plans.js`. Snap 50–180s every 5s. Do not reuse mile 300–900 clamp.
- New runners: mileage first, no 4×4 until they can jog 10 min.
- Intermediate+: one 4×4 per week. Hills for speed. Injury map: shin/Achilles → no hills.
