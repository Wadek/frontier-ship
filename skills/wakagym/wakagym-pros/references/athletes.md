# Athletes behind the adapted sessions

Facts only. Volume in the app is always scaled. Do not prescribe their mileage.

- **Josh Kerr** — Scottish 1500/mile. Project 222: 4×(200–600–200) in the Albuquerque build before the July 2026 London 3:42.66 mile. Lower mileage than many rivals; strength twice a week. Slot: mile sets.
- **Marius Bakken** — Norwegian 5k (13:06). Published the double-threshold “Norwegian model”: 45/15 and lactate-guided threshold, not redline VO2 every day. Slot: 45/15.
- **Jakob Ingebrigtsen** — Olympic 1500, Norwegian double-threshold. COROS logs: 25×400 short rest, 4×6 min treadmill threshold, 2×10 uphill 200s. Do not copy his weekly hours. Slots: rhythm 400s, threshold blocks, hill repeats.
- **Eliud Kipchoge** — Marathon WR lineage (Berlin 2017 2:03:32 log). Kaptagat 2400m: 13×1k, 30×1 min fartlek, 2k/1k pairs, 4×10 min rolling, 40k long. We use the *shape* at club volume. Slots: rolling tempo, minute fartlek.
- **Andreas Almgren** — Swedish 5k/10k (Valencia 10k 26:45, 12:44 5k). 6×6 min threshold, 20×400, 3×3k, 10×1k, mixed sharpening ladder. Slot: six-minute repeats.
- **Grant Fisher** — US 5k/10k. Bookend session (long–800s–long) and 4×2 mile. Slot: bookends.
- **Nick Willis** — NZ 1500 Olympic medalist. Michigan (Warhurst): track reps alternating with road tempo, fast 400 closer. Slot: Michigan cut.
- **Faith Kipyegon** — Kenyan 1500 WR. World Athletics feature: 2k → 1ks → 400s at Kaptagat. Slot: progression track.
- **Sifan Hassan** — Dutch 1500-to-marathon. Interview format: 15 min + 20 min tempo then 4×300. Slot: tempo then 300s.
- **Mo Farah** — UK 5k/10k Olympic champion. 2015 Worlds ladder 1600 down to 200 with lap jogs. Slot: descending ladder.
- **Hicham El Guerrouj** — Moroccan mile WR (3:43.13, 1999) until 2026. 10×400 speed endurance. Slot: speed 400s.
- **Steve Moneghetti** — Australian marathoner. Mona fartlek (2×90, 4×60, 4×30, 4×15 with equal recoveries). Slot: Mona fartlek.
- **Emil Zátopek** — Czech 5k/10k/marathon Olympic sweep 1952. Huge 200/400 days; we take a small slice. Slot: short intervals.
- **Renato Canova** — Italian coach. 4×5k with 1k floats is marathon-specific; too big for Muumi. Do not rotate onto a mile week.
- **Arthur Lydiard** — NZ coach. Hill circuit: uphill strength, jog, downhill stride. Slot: hill circuit.
- **Mihály Iglói** — Hungarian/US interval coach. Morning ladders of mixed short reps. Slot: morning ladder (expert).
- **David Rudisha** — Kenyan 800 WR. 400/300 speed primer. Slot: 400/300 primer.
- **Paula Radcliffe** — Women’s marathon WR era. 5–6 miles at half-marathon goal pace. Slot: half-marathon tempo (minutes, not miles).
- **Keely Hodgkinson** — English 800 Olympic champion. Short hills for speed. Slot: short hills.
- **Kenenisa Bekele** — Ethiopian 5k/10k WR, marathon. 400/200 pairs (Science of Running). Slot: 400/200 pairs.
- **Haile Gebrselassie** — Ethiopian 10k/marathon. 3×2k track. Slot: 2k repeats.
- **Grete Waitz** — Norwegian marathon. Kilometre repeats. Slot: kilometre repeats.
- **Bernard Lagat** — Kenyan-American 1500/5k. Two sets of 400s. Slot: two sets of 400s.
- **Joan Benoit Samuelson** — 1984 Olympic marathon. Flow miles on the long run. Slot: flow miles (advanced+).
- **Mara Yamauchi** — British marathoner. Ten-second hill efforts. Slot: ten-second hills.
